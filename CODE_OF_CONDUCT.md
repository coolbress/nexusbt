# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews and
discussions, is expected to:

- be respectful and considerate, and assume good faith;
- keep disagreement about the work, not the person;
- accept review and give it constructively;
- refrain from harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and the
  publishing of others' private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests
that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently exclude
anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

## Reporting

Report a concern to the maintainers privately through the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form, marking it as a conduct report,
rather than in a public issue. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>).
